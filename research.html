BETTER QUESTIONS, BETTER OBJECTS

Research for
real life.

Sustainability is not a single claim. It is a series of choices we keep examining.

01 / DESIGN FOR LONGEVITY

Can it be repaired?

We consider joinery, replaceable components, care instructions, and access to spare parts when evaluating a product. Keeping furniture useful for longer is a practical part of reducing waste.

02 / MATERIAL CHOICES

Look beyond the surface.

We assess durability, sourcing information, finish chemistry where data is available, packaging, and end-of-life options—not just the appearance of a material.

03 / USE

Designed around people

Comfort, stability, cleaning, and daily usability belong in the sustainability conversation.

04 / TRANSPARENCY

Claims need evidence

We aim to publish specific, verifiable product details and avoid vague environmental promises.

05 / NEXT LIFE

Keep materials in use

Repair, resale, refurbishment, and responsible recycling can all help extend value.
